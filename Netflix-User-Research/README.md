# Netflix User Research & Customer Persona Case Study

A UX research case study on Netflix: how users discover, select, watch, and manage content, and what design opportunities emerge from the research.

## ⚠️ Data Disclosure
The interview data, findings, empathy maps, and personas in this repository are **synthesized examples**, not results from real conducted interviews — built to demonstrate the full research pipeline end to end. Full detail and replacement instructions are in [`Research/Research_Objectives.md`](Research/Research_Objectives.md). Before submitting this as a genuine research deliverable, replace the example data with your own interviews.
