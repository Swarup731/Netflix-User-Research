## Insight 1 — Choice Overload
Users find it difficult to decide what to watch because of the huge content library. Several participants describe spending more time browsing than watching.

## Insight 2 — Recommendation Fatigue
Some users feel that recommendations repeatedly show similar movies/shows, reducing trust that the algorithm understands their taste.

## Insight 3 — Discovery Takes Time
Users often spend several minutes scrolling before selecting something, especially when they don't have a specific title in mind.

## Insight 4 — Search Could Be More Flexible
Users want to search by criteria Netflix's current search doesn't fully support:
- Mood
- Genre
- Actor
- Language
- Duration
- Rating
- Theme

## Insight 5 — Pricing Matters
Some users factor subscription cost into how often they use Netflix, and cost is a recurring reason cited for considering cancellation.
