# Interview Questionnaire — Netflix User Research

Ask actual Netflix users these questions. Target duration: 10–15 minutes.

## General Usage
1. How often do you use Netflix?
2. Which device do you primarily use for Netflix?
3. What type of content do you usually watch?
4. How long do you typically spend deciding what to watch?

## Content Discovery
5. How do you normally find something new to watch?
6. Do Netflix recommendations usually match your interests?
7. Have you ever spent a long time browsing without finding something to watch?
8. Do you use Netflix's search function? How useful is it?

## User Experience
9. What do you like most about the Netflix app?
10. What frustrates you most about Netflix?
11. Do you use My List/Watchlist?
12. Do you find Netflix's categories easy to understand?

## Subscription
13. How do you feel about Netflix's subscription pricing?
14. Does the price influence how often you use Netflix?
15. Would you consider cancelling Netflix? Why?

## Improvement
16. If you could change one thing about Netflix, what would it be?
17. What feature would make Netflix more useful to you?
18. Would you like more personalized recommendations?
