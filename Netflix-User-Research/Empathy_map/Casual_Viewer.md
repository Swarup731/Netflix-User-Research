# Empathy Map — "The Casual Viewer"

**Says**
> "I only watch Netflix when I have free time."

**Thinks**
- I want something easy to watch.
- I don't want to spend 20 minutes searching.

**Does**
- Watches popular content
- Searches occasionally
- Uses recommendations
- Watches movies rather than long series

**Feels**
- Relaxed when watching
- Frustrated when nothing interests them
- Satisfied when recommendations are accurate
