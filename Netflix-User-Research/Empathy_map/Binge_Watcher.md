# Empathy Map — "The Binge Watcher"

**Says**
> "I spend more time deciding what to watch than actually watching."

**Thinks**
- There are too many options.
- I want Netflix to understand my taste.
- I don't want to watch trailers for everything.

**Does**
- Scrolls through recommendations
- Checks ratings
- Searches for actors
- Adds shows to My List
- Watches multiple episodes

**Feels**
- Excited when discovering a good show
- Frustrated when recommendations aren't relevant
- Overwhelmed by too many choices
